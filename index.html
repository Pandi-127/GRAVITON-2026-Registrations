Jaya Sakthi Engineering College

Accredited with NAAC 'A' Grade, approved by AICTE New Delhi, and affiliated to Anna University Chennai. Located in Thiruninravur, Chennai, Jaya Sakthi Engineering College stands as a beacon of academic excellence and technical innovation.

  • State-of-the-art AI & Cyber Security Research Labs
  • Industry-aligned Technical Curriculum & Training
  • Vibrant Student Innovation & Hackathon Culture

About GRAVITON 2026

GRAVITON is the flagship National Level Technical Symposium hosted by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering and Cyber Security. It provides a high-octane battleground for visionaries, coders, prompt engineers, and gamers across the nation.
